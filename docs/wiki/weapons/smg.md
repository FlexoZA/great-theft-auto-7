# SMG

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Weapons](README.md) / SMG

<img src="img/smg.png" alt="SMG" width="320">

Fifteen 9mm rounds a second, tight and quick to reload, but spent by about 500 px. Fires the pistol's 9mm rounds.

**How to use it:** Drag it into a weapon slot; its number key picks it.

## How to get it

- Won first: once you have picked one up, the shop sells it to you, in every tier.
