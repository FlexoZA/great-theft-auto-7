# Suppressor

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/suppressors-suppressor.png" alt="Suppressor" width="240">

The Combine's heavy: a minigun at the hip and a blue energy shield round it. Slow on its feet and slow to turn, it holds a post and hoses anyone who walks into its sights.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Citadel, holding the gallery and the reactor deck |
| **Health** | 220 |
| **Shield** | 160, soaked before the health goes |
| **Speed** | 42 px/s walking, 34 closing in while it fires; turns at 1.5 rad/s |
| **Sight** | 640 px in a 60 degree cone, 150 px all round |
| **Drops** | 12 koins, and sometimes a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Minigun**: 5 damage a round, about 12 rounds a second once the barrels turn (0.9 s to spin up), 3.2 s of fire before it vents

## Abilities

- The shield soaks every hit first. It comes back 5 s after the last hit, 30 points a second.
- After 3.2 s of fire the gun vents for 1.8 s: no rounds while it does.
- Loses you and keeps hosing where it saw you last for 2 s, then walks over and searches for 5 s.
- Never goes more than 520 px from its post, and stops closing in at 260 px.
- Hears a shot within 480 px, and turns towards a hit from out of sight.
- Too heavy to scare: a stink cloud doesn't move it.

## How to beat it

- Step out of cover while it vents: the barrels wind down and nothing comes out.
- Shock tears the shield down 2 times as fast.
- Run round it: it turns at only 1.5 rad/s and can't keep up with a sprint.
- Keep hitting it: the shield only comes back after 5 s without a hit.
- A rocket or grenade takes 45 off it, shield first.
- A freeze holds it like anyone.
