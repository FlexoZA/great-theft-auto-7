# Crazy Karen

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Karen's Cul-de-sac

<img src="img/karen-boss.png" alt="Crazy Karen" width="240">

The first boss. She stands in her turning circle ranting about nothing, and the moment anyone comes near she charges them and slaps whatever she reaches, car or walker.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Karen's Cul-de-sac, in the turning circle at the end of her street |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 1500 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 75 hits |
| **Speed** | 165 px/s charging, 55 winded |
| **Sight** | 650 px |
| **Drops** | 30 koins, and an uzi for every player |

## Weapons

- **Slap**: 15 melee damage, one every 1.3 s, to whatever she reaches, car or walker
- **Scream**: 20 impact damage to everyone within 120 px of where she aimed it, and your screen swims for 3 s

## Abilities

- Every 8 s she plants her feet and screams at whoever is within 520 px: a ring on the ground shows where, and it lands 1.2 s later.
- Charging costs her breath: about 6 s flat out and she is winded, down to a 55 px/s walk with no breath to scream.
- Her simps turn up while she stands, up to 5 at a time.
- Under 40% health she marches off to the nearest medkit within 700 px and gets 200 health back from it.
- Stomps back to her turning circle when nobody is about.

## How to beat it

- Step out of the ring before the scream lands.
- Run her dry: winded, she can't keep up with anyone sprinting.
- Ramming her at 60 px/s or more hurts her (more the faster you go), but the car loses 12 health and bounces off.
- A freeze roots her to the spot, and a stink sends her running.
- Grab the medkits before she does.

---

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
