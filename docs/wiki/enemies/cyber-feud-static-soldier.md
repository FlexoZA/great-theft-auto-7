# Static ganger

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-static-soldier.png" alt="Static ganger" width="240">

The Static's street gang: dark mismatched clothes, cyan-tipped cable hair, a chrome forearm. All noise: a wide cone, a keen ear and packs that rush.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Drop's corners and blocks, the Market's west and its chop shop, the Overpass's shoulders and the Knot, Halcyon Tower breaking in from below |
| **Health** | 50 |
| **Speed** | 62 px/s walking, 48 on a beat, 90 closing in |
| **Sight** | 640 px |
| **Numbers** | Packs of 4; more on a job finished before |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shotgun**: 6 pellets of 11 damage a pull, one at a time, 1.1 s apart; 3 in 5 of them carry one
- **Pistol**: 20 damage a round, in bursts of 3, then 0.8 s; 2 in 5 of them carry one
- **MG (a station's nest)**: 12 damage a round, 0.08 s apart, in bursts of 14 then 1.4 s, out to 700 px, only into the nest's arc

## Abilities

- Calm until somebody takes the Shard or a player hurts one of them (on the Drop), or a player walks in past the way in (the Market, the tower, the Roof): then the whole street wakes at once.
- Sees 640 px ahead in a cone 80 degrees wide (120 once on edge) and hears anyone within 230 px all round.
- Spotting somebody, shouts it and calls in up to 3 of his own side within 800 px.
- One of them going down sends up to 4 more within 700 px to look.
- Guard stations: an MG nest with a gunner on it and riflemen round it who fight from cover; drop the gunner and the nearest of the crew runs to the gun. The station house's garrison (3 of them for one player) comes out of the door one after another once you are within 650 px of it.
- The feud: on the Drop and the Market the two sides shoot each other on sight. On the Overpass they hold fire until a round of one side hits the other, then fight for 25 s after the last such hit. In the tower and on the Roof they never do: their rounds pass through each other.
- In Halcyon Tower they break in from below: a pack up onto the lowest floor anyone is on, the first 12 s after it wakes and then every 35 s, while fewer than 8 of them (4 more for each human past the first) are on that floor. From floor 2 up a sparker comes with them, from floor 3 up a brute.
- In the Market, General Volt's backup: a pack runs in from the lanes each time he shouts for it.

## How to beat it

- He turns at only 2 rad/s: sprint across his cone and he loses you.
- Pull them into the other side's line of fire and let them fight it out.
- A car at speed runs them down.
