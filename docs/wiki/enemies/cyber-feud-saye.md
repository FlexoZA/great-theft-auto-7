# Director Saye

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-saye.png" alt="Director Saye" width="240">

Halcyon's director: calm, precise, a grey suit over the best hardware money can buy. She fights alongside Kilowatt, at range, and never runs.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Roof, by the billboard, from the moment it wakes |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 2000 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s walking (95 in phase 2), 38 winded |
| **Sight** | 760 px |
| **Drops** | 40 koins and 2 pickups |

## Weapons

- **Sightline**: one heavy round of 45 damage at 1800 px/s down a thin magenta line that shows for 1 s first (0.5 in phase 2), every 3.2 s (2.4 in phase 2); in phase 2 two at once, in a V
- **Executive drones**: 3 orbit her, shooting whoever is nearest (their own page)
- **Restructuring**: every 14 s she drops a sentry turret where she stands and walks away from it, up to 3 of hers at once

## Abilities

- Keeps 420 px between herself and whoever she has.
- Calls her drones back up 12 s after losing them.
- Stand within 200 px of Kilowatt and her sightline finds you, quicker.
- While Kilowatt vents, she parks her drones round him: while any are there, hits on him do no extra damage.
- With Kilowatt down, a hostile takeover: 6 drones, and her sightline fires 3 rounds, 0.15 s apart.
- Phase 2 at half health: her skin cracks, the sightline takes half as long and fires in a V, and she walks faster.
- Goes for a medkit when badly hurt, dodges abilities about to land on her, and runs out of breath.
- Only players' rounds reach her.

## How to beat it

- Step off the magenta line before it fires.
- Close the gap: she walks, never runs, and her turrets stay where she dropped them.
- Shooting her makes Kilowatt come for you: have a friend keep him busy.
