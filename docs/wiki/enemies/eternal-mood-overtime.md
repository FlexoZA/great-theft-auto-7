# Overtime guy

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-overtime.png" alt="Overtime guy" width="240">

The big man with the chaingun, who never went home. He holds his ground near his post and fights from cover, spinning up each time he steps out.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Fobos Base by the hangar lift, Heck on Earth's overpass and starport gate, and the Foundry |
| **Health** | 100 |
| **Speed** | 35 px/s, and he never chases |
| **Sight** | 650 px ahead, 80 degrees across (100 on edge), 180 px all round |
| **Drops** | 6 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Minigun**: winds up for 0.6 s (you hear it), then 6 damage a round, 8 rounds a second, out to 650 px

## Abilities

- Never goes more than 260 px from his post: he fights from round it, in and out of cover.
- Slow to swing round: 1.6 rad/s, so his stream drags after you.
- Goes to ground with the rest when shot from out of sight, but never out on a sweep.
- Spotting you, it groans: the horde within 380 px comes to look. One going down sends those within 520 px to where he fell.
- Its rounds pass through the rest of Heck: nothing of Heck's hurts anything else of Heck's.

## How to beat it

- Listen for the wind-up and get behind something before the rounds come.
- Run across his aim, not away from it: he can't turn fast enough to follow.
- He won't chase you: stay past 910 px from his post and pick him off.
