# The Runner

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / The city

<img src="img/events-runner.png" alt="The Runner" width="240">

A man in a tracksuit sprinting the streets at three times your sprint. He goes after nobody, but whatever is in his lane pays for it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The city, as an event: he comes onto a crossing away from everyone |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 1200 |
| **Speed** | 510 px/s flat out, 45 winded |
| **Drops** | 40 koins, and his second wind in a random tier (legendary 3 times in a hundred) ([second wind](../abilities/secondwind.md)) |

## Weapons

- **Trample**: 30 impact damage to a player on foot in his way, once every 1 s
- **Running into a car**: wrecks it on the spot, whatever the car; the driver bails out

## Abilities

- Keeps to the streets, in one lane or the other, picking a new street at every crossing.
- Gibs any pedestrian he runs through.
- Under 40% health he leaves the street for a medkit within 1200 px, which puts back 200.
- He has a huge lungful, about 30 s flat out, then he is winded and walks for about 4 s.

## How to beat it

- Catch him when he is winded: that is your window, and the boss bar says so.
- Keep out of his lane, on foot or in a car.
- A freeze stops him dead; a stink ahead of him turns him round.

---

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players._
