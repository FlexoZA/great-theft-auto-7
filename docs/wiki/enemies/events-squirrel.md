# Squirrel (Bigfoot's)

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / The city

<img src="img/events-squirrel.png" alt="Squirrel (Bigfoot's)" width="240">

Bigfoot's litter. They sit a moment at his feet, then shoot off at the nearest player or building, far faster than anyone runs, and burst on whatever they reach.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The city, round Bigfoot's feet while his event is on |
| **Health** | 10: one hit of anything |
| **Speed** | 340 px/s |
| **Sight** | 1000 px |
| **Numbers** | 15 at a time for one player, more with more players |

## Weapons

- **Burst**: 3 melee damage to whoever it reaches, 6 to a building

## Abilities

- Swerves after you, but overshoots a sharp dodge until it is within 140 px.
- Bursts on its own after 7 s.

## How to beat it

- Dodge sideways at the last moment, or shoot it: one round does it.
- Freezing and stink work on them.
