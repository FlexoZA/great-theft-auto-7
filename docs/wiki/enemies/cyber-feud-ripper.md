# Ripper

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-ripper.png" alt="Ripper" width="240">

The Static's knife fighters: lean, with two chrome blades out of the forearms. Fast, and always after whoever is most alone.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Market's west lanes, walking the Static's beats |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s on its beat, 150 running in, 560 in a lunge |
| **Sight** | 600 px, a 110 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 260 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Lunge**: 28 melee damage to whoever is on its line, about 224 px long, every 1.8 s
- **Slash**: 10 melee damage within 26 px, every 0.55 s

## Abilities

- Goes for whoever is most alone: anyone else within 260 px of you makes you a worse pick.
- From 200 px off, crouches for 0.5 s with a cyan line on the ground showing where it will go, then lunges down it. The line is set once it crouches.
- Up close it slashes 2 times, then backs off for 0.7 s to lunge again.
- Gives up 3 s after it last saw you and goes back to its beat.
- In the feud it is the Static's: it goes for Halcyon's officers and enforcers too.

## How to beat it

- Step sideways off the cyan line while it crouches: the lunge goes where the line was.
- Stay near your friends: it picks on whoever is alone.
- Shoot it while it catches its breath (0.6 s) after a lunge.
- An enforcer's shield stops its blades from in front.
