# Intern

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-intern.png" alt="Intern" width="240">

The UAC's unpaid interns, possessed and unarmed, the lowest rank there is. Never one at a time: they come in mobs, crouched round a body they dragged in until something wakes them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Fobos Base, Heck on Earth and the Foundry, in mobs crouched round a meal; the Floor Manager's meetings bring more |
| **Health** | 20: 1 pistol round |
| **Speed** | 85 px/s from afar, 140 within 200 px |
| **Numbers** | A mob of several for one player, one more for each player past the first |
| **Drops** | 1 koin, and now and then (15%) a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Claws**: 7 melee damage, one every 1 s, right up against you (arms up for 0.35 s first)

## Abilities

- A mob wakes when you come within about 420 px, it sees you in plain sight within 520 px, or one of it is hurt. Then the whole mob comes.
- Awake, each one goes for the nearest player within 1600 px.
- A Mecha Zombie walks a loop round each mob, and its scream wakes the mob too.

## How to beat it

- Shoot one and the lot of them come: thin the mob from a distance, or throw a grenade into the meal.
- The blood trail tells you where a mob is eating before you walk into it.
- One claw is nothing; a crowd round you is not. Back off as you shoot.
