# Zombie staffer

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-staffer.png" alt="Zombie staffer" width="240">

The UAC's office staff, Heck's now, still mostly at work. A staffer shambles at whoever it sees and fires its pistol now and then: no cover, no tactics.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Fobos Base, Heck on Earth and the Foundry, at desks and posts and on patrol |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s after you, 40 on patrol |
| **Sight** | 520 px ahead, 70 degrees across (90 on edge), 140 px all round |
| **Drops** | 2 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Pistol**: 8 damage a round, 0.7 s on you before the first, then one every 1.2 to 1.9 s, out to 520 px

## Abilities

- Closes to about 260 px and stands there shooting.
- Spotting you, it groans: the horde within 380 px comes to look. One going down sends those within 520 px to where he fell.
- Shot by somebody it can't see, it turns that way and comes.
- Loses you and looks about where it last saw you for 6 s, then goes back to its post.
- Its rounds pass through the rest of Heck: nothing of Heck's hurts anything else of Heck's.

## How to beat it

- The easiest of the possessed: it never takes cover, so cut it down in the open.
- The Floor Manager's quick meetings bring more of them in through the Hangar's doorways.
