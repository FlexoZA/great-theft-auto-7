# Rocket hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-rpg.png" alt="Rocket hauler" width="240">

A heavier hauler with a launcher on one arm and two pods of rockets on its back. It hangs back and makes you move.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Standing watch from far back: under Liar's Landing's gantries, then Prosperity, the Assembly Line and Thousand Papercuts |
| **Health** | 120 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s on its beat, 90 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 760 px, a 80 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 170 px |
| **Drops** | 5 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Launcher**: one slow rocket every 2.4 to 3.4 s, 420 px/s, 30 explosive damage within 90 px
- **Pods**: every 7 to 10 s its pods glow for 1 s, then 4 rockets come down on red rings round you, 28 explosive damage each within 80 px

## Abilities

- Keeps well back: closes in past 650 px, backs off inside 400.
- Seeing somebody, it calls the army within 520 px to come and look.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- When its pods glow, keep moving: one rocket always lands right where you stand.
- Its own rockets are slow enough to sidestep.
