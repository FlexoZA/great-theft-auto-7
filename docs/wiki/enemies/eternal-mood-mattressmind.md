# The Spider Mattressmind

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-mattressmind.png" alt="The Spider Mattressmind" width="240">

A giant brain on four mechanical spider legs with a chaingun under its chin, standing on a mattress it drags everywhere. The Snooze Gate stays locked until it is down.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Heck, on the Mattress's plateau before the Snooze Gate; it wakes when you come up onto the plateau |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 5200 |
| **Armour** | the mattress: a round from in front does only 30% of its damage |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s, any way on its legs, 35 winded |
| **Sight** | 950 px |
| **Drops** | 60 koins, and maybe a pickup or two |

## Weapons

- **Chaingun**: spins up for 0.8 s (a whine, the barrels glowing), then 2.4 s of fire, a round every 0.09 s, 6 damage each, out to about 760 px; the aim drags after you at 1.25 rad/s. Every 2.4 to 3.2 s (1.8 to 2.4 under half health)
- **Slam**: within 140 px: legs up for 0.6 s (the reach shows on the floor), then 32 impact damage to everyone within 135 px, knocked 160 px; at most every 2.4 s
- **Yawn**: under half health, every 8 to 11 s: for 2.2 s it pulls everyone within 650 px in at 95 px/s, then snaps shut: 45 melee damage to whoever is within 140 px, spat out 200 px

## Abilities

- Keeps about 420 px from you, turning to face you, and keeps to the plateau.
- At two thirds and a third of its health it murmurs "...five more minutes...": 3 imps and 1 Cacodaemon come up onto the plateau (more with more players).
- Under 40% health it goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- Walking flat out costs it breath, and each burst costs more: about 13 s of walking and it's winded, creeping.
- Gets out from under abilities.

## How to beat it

- Get round the mattress: shooting it in the face is a waste of rounds.
- Run across the chaingun's aim: it can't turn fast enough to follow.
- Sprint against a yawn: a walk won't hold you, a sprint will.
- A freeze holds it stiff.
