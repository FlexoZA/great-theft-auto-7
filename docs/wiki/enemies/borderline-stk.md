# Stalker hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-stk.png" alt="Stalker hauler" width="240">

Hyperbole's hunter: tall and quick, two long legs in front and a wheel behind, a flechette pod on each shoulder. It dodges what you shoot at it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Liar's Landing: a pair hunting the container stacks, and one in the last wave at the ferry lift |
| **Health** | 140 |
| **Speed** | 90 px/s on its beat, 190 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 640 px, a 100 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 200 px |
| **Drops** | 6 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Flechettes**: bursts of 6, 0.07 s apart, 1.8 to 2.6 s between bursts; each sticks in you for 3 bullet damage, then blows a second later: 10 within 40 px
- **Charge**: now and then at somebody 110 to 380 px off: it crouches for 0.5 s, its pods and eye glowing, then runs straight at where they stood for 20 impact damage

## Abilities

- Once it knows you are about, it throws itself aside from a round about to hit it. A shot from hiding lands.
- Each dodge and charge costs breath, shown under it; a few in a row and it is winded: walking, no dodge or charge until it has 50 breath back.
- Under 40% health it backs off to a medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Open up on it before it sees you: those shots can't be dodged.
- Make it dodge until it is winded (its breath goes red), then unload.
- Step aside when it crouches: it can't turn once it runs.
- Pick up the medkits near it first.
