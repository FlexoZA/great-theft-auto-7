# Kamikaze hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-exp.png" alt="Kamikaze hauler" width="240">

Stripped to the frame, no arms, a red core in its chest. It runs at you and goes off.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | In packs of three from Prosperity on |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Speed** | 150 px/s at you |
| **Sight** | 520 px, a 110 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 160 px |
| **Drops** | 2 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Core**: goes off within 60 px of you: 45 explosive damage within 120 px

## Abilities

- Its core blinks faster from 220 px off.
- Killed, it goes off all the same where it fell, and that blast hurts its own side too.
- It has no legs to lose: no crawl, it just blows.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it while it is still among its friends.
- A sprint only just keeps ahead of it: shoot, don't run.
