# Dread Knight

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-dread-knight.png" alt="Dread Knight" width="240">

Heck's elite: a head taller than you in black plate cracked through with red, a horned helm and a long blade grown out of each forearm. Not a boss, but the strongest and smartest thing in the horde.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Heck on Earth, the Foundry and Heck, each standing at his spot until you come near |
| **Health** | 600 |
| **Speed** | 85 px/s walking, 150 running at you from well off, 50 winded |
| **Sight** | 600 px, no wall in between; he wakes within 480 px; awake, he hunts the nearest player within 1400 px |
| **Drops** | 8 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Slash**: within 46 px: blade back for 0.35 s (his reach shows on the floor), then 26 melee damage to everyone in front of him, at most one every 1 s
- **Lunge**: from 90 to 230 px off: crouched for 0.4 s (a lane on the floor), then a dash at 560 px/s for up to 0.4 s, aimed where you're going, that slashes whoever he reaches; one every 2.5 to 3.5 s

## Abilities

- Whatever lands knocks you back 150 px, stuns you for 1.2 s (less what you wear against melee) and makes you bleed; 70% of the time it's a deep wound too: 6 damage a second for 6 s, until a medkit.
- He lets whoever he stunned get up, then lunges within 0.6 s of it if he has the breath.
- Dodges: a round about to hit him and he throws himself off its line, at most every 1.2 s, and only with breath to spare. He gets out from under abilities too.
- Comes at you weaving, and backs off to about 220 px to get his breath back when winded.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px.

## How to beat it

- Watch for the crouch: the lane on the floor is where he'll go, so step off it.
- Run him dry: lunges and dodges cost breath, and winded he can't dodge your rounds.
- Hit him while he stands after a slash or a lunge: he's open for 0.45 s.
- A freeze holds him stiff.
