# Sting drone

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-sting.png" alt="Sting drone" width="240">

Halcyon's attack drone: small, fast and armed, flying in swarms that make strafing runs through where you are going, faster than a car.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Swarms over the Overpass's gantries, and in Halcyon Tower's server room |
| **Health** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 560 px/s on a run (340 indoors), faster than a car |
| **Sight** | A player within 700 px wakes the swarm (560 indoors) |
| **Numbers** | 2 swarms of 3 for one player, 1 more swarm for each human past the first |
| **Drops** | 3 koins |

## Weapons

- **Light gun**: 6 damage a round, in bursts of 3 (0.11 s apart), then 0.7 s, out to 420 px, when you are ahead of it

## Abilities

- Each run aims 0.45 s ahead of where you are going, passes 70 px to one side of you and carries on 260 px past before it loops back.
- Gives up past 1500 px (1100 indoors) and flies home.
- In the tower a swarm keeps to its own room and only fires with a clear line.
- A sparker's EMP knocks one out for 5 s.
- Only players' rounds reach them.

## How to beat it

- Shoot them as they come in straight at you: a single pistol round drops one.
- Change direction as a run comes in: they aim at where you were going.
