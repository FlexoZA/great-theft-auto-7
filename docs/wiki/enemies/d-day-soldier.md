# D-Day soldier

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / D-Day

<img src="img/d-day-soldier.png" alt="D-Day soldier" width="240">

The Major's men. Guards sweep a cone of sight over the beach from their posts; riflemen come out of the barracks doors and walk down the hill at you. Both open fire on anyone they spot.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Looz'er Beach: guards in the bunkers, trenches and huts, riflemen out of the barracks |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 2 hits |
| **Speed** | 62 px/s walking down the hill, 90 closing in |
| **Sight** | 640 px in a 30 degree cone, and 150 px all round |
| **Numbers** | 16 guards and up to 4 riflemen at once for one player, a rifleman every 7 s |
| **Drops** | 1 koin, and sometimes something to pick up |

## Weapons

- **AK-47**: 18 damage a round, a round every 0.6 s while he can see you, out to 640 px
- **Hand grenade**: 10% of them carry 1 each: 80 explosive damage across 100 px, thrown 140 to 400 px

## Abilities

- Spots you, turns to follow you at 115 degrees a second and fires after 0.7 s to take aim.
- Calls in the nearest 3 within 800 px when he spots you; one going down brings everyone within 700 px to look.
- Comes after you when you duck out of sight (up to 900 px from where he started) and searches where he lost you for 5 s.
- Shot at from out of sight, he and his mates take cover and wait, then send a squad to look.
- Behind them, mortars fall on the beach every 1.4 to 3 s: a ring shows where for 1.3 s, then up to 45 explosive damage across 95 px.

## How to beat it

- Keep a tank stopper or a sandbag wall between you and the cones of sight.
- Sprint across a cone: he can't turn fast enough to follow you.
- A freeze stops them looking and shooting, and a stink sends them running.
