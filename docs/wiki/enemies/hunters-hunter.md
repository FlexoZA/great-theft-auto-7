# Hunter

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/hunters-hunter.png" alt="Hunter" width="240">

The Combine's tripod hunter: quick on three legs, it keeps its distance, zaps you with shock rounds and throws itself aside from whatever you send at it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | City 17 round the Citadel, the Winding Road and the Citadel; the Hunter-Chopper and A-Man's finale bring more in |
| **Health** | 180 |
| **Speed** | 85 px/s on its beat, 150 running, 115 strafing, 430 in a dodge |
| **Sight** | 640 px in a 90 degree cone, 170 px all round |
| **Drops** | 8 koins, and sometimes a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shock rounds**: 8 shock damage a round in bursts of 5, 1.4 s between bursts, out to 918 px; the shock runs on through you for 3 s at 8 damage a second
- **Stun shot**: 10 shock damage and holds you still for 1.2 s; it charges for 0.6 s first, about every 8 s

## Abilities

- Keeps you between 260 and 440 px: backs off when you rush it, closes in when you run.
- Dodges rounds and abilities it sees coming, no more than once every 1.1 s and only with the breath for it.
- Under 45% health it runs for a medkit (70 back) or an energy drink (30 back, and quicker for 6 s) within 700 px.
- Calls every Hunter within 1000 px when it starts a fight or is shot at.
- Hears a shot within 550 px, and turns on whoever fires a round past it or into it.
- It tires: running and dodging spend its breath (the blue bar under it), and a winded one can only walk.

## How to beat it

- Its stun shot goes down the line it shows while charging: step off the line.
- Wear it out: a winded Hunter can't dodge, and walks slower than its patrol.
- Pick up the medkits and drinks near a fight before it does.
- A teleport lands at once: it can't see that coming. A freeze holds it and stops a charge.
