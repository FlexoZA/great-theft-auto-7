# Poison headcrab

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/poison-zombie-headcrab.png" alt="Poison headcrab" width="240">

What rides on the Poison Zombie. He throws them at you; they crawl after you and leap, and a bite poisons you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Winding Road's pass, thrown off the Poison Zombie's back |
| **Health** | 30 |

| **Sight** | 1100 px |

| **Drops** | 1 koin |

## Weapons

- **Bite**: 35 poison damage, and you're poisoned for 6 s (4 damage a second)
- **Scratch**: 15 damage to a car it lands on

## Abilities

- Thrown, it bites whoever it lands on.
- On the ground it leaps at you from 150 px (70 from a car's side).
- Rests 1.3 s after a leap, 2.2 after a bite, then comes again.

## How to beat it

- A sprint gets away from one; a walk doesn't.
- Drive over one at 60 px/s or more and it's squashed.
- A medkit cures the poison.
- Shoot it while it rests after a leap.
