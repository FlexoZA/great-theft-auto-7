# Antlion

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/antlions-antlion.png" alt="Antlion" width="240">

Insects out of the sand. A swarm lies buried until you come near, then bursts up, runs you down from all sides and bites.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Coast, buried in swarms in the sand; A-Man's briefcase calls them up in the Citadel |
| **Health** | 36 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 2 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 150 px/s running, 360 leaping |
| **Sight** | a swarm wakes when you come within 320 px, and chases you from 1400 px |
| **Numbers** | A swarm at each spot on the sand, bigger with more players |
| **Drops** | 1 koin, now and then something to pick up |

## Weapons

- **Bite**: 5 melee damage, then 0.75 s before the next
- **Leap**: from 110 to 240 px away it sometimes leaps the rest of the way, landing ready to bite

## Abilities

- Takes 0.9 s to crawl up out of the sand, and can be shot while it does.
- Spreads out round you rather than queueing up, and finds its way round anything in the way.
- Nobody to go after for 6 s and it burrows back under, to wake again the same way.
- The Combine's rounds pass by them, so the bunkers' guns don't thin them out for you.

## How to beat it

- A sprint gets away from them, slowly.
- Back off as a swarm comes up and shoot them on the way out of the sand.
- A freeze stiffens one, and a stink sends it scuttling off.
