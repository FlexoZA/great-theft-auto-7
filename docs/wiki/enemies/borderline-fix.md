# Medic drone

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-fix.png" alt="Medic drone" width="240">

A small yellow disc on four rotors with a red cross. It never fights: it heals the most hurt hauler near it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Hanging behind the haulers: Liar's Landing (and its lift's waves), Prosperity, the Refinery and the Assembly Line |
| **Health** | 25 |
| **Speed** | 150 px/s |
| **Drops** | 1 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Abilities

- Heals the hauler it tends by 12 a second along its beam, 150 px long.
- Looks for somebody to tend within 700 px, and hangs 60 px behind them, out of your line of fire.
- Flies over walls, and anything can shoot it.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it first: it goes down fast, and what it tends goes down slow until it does.
- Get round the side of whoever it tends to get a clear shot.
