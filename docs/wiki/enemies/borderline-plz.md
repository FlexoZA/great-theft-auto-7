# Plasma hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-plz.png" alt="Plasma hauler" width="240">

A cannon on its arm with purple coils down it, and a tank of Bragium on its back to feed it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Standing watch on the Bragium Pit's rings, then Prosperity, the Refinery and the Assembly Line |
| **Health** | 90 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s on its beat, 105 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 600 px, a 80 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 160 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Plasma cannon**: its coils glow for 0.6 s, then one slow bolt, 260 px/s, every 1.4 to 2.2 s: 22 plasma damage, splashing 40 px; plasma melts armor

## Abilities

- Its tank is its weak spot: killed by a hit from behind, it bursts, 40 plasma damage within 95 px, its own side included.
- Seeing somebody, it calls the army within 520 px to come and look.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- A sprint sidesteps the bolt: move when the coils glow.
- Finish it from behind when it stands among its friends.
