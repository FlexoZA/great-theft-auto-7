# Enforcer

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-enforcer.png" alt="Enforcer" width="240">

Halcyon's riot police: darker grey armour behind a riot shield with a magenta edge. Slow, tough, and planted in a choke point.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Market's south gates, in front of the Overpass's roadblocks, Halcyon Tower's guard and executive floors, and the Roof with the dropship |
| **Health** | 120 |
| **Armour** | A riot shield: stops every round within 70 degrees of straight on (blasts and fire go through) |
| **Speed** | 34 px/s stepping up, 46 going back; turns at 1.3 rad/s |
| **Sight** | 560 px, a 70 degree cone (110 once he has somebody), and he hears anyone within 160 px |
| **Drops** | 6 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shotgun**: 6 pellets of 9 damage a pull, a pull every 1.4 s, out to about 230 px

## Abilities

- Holds his post: steps up on whoever he has, never more than 300 px from it, and stops 150 px off them to shoot.
- Loses you and looks round where you were for 4 s, then walks back to his post.
- Shot from where he isn't looking, he turns that way.
- In the feud he is Halcyon's: he shoots the Static, and his shield stops their rounds and blades too.

## How to beat it

- Get round his side while he turns: at 1.3 rad/s, he is slow to face you.
- Blow him up or burn him: explosives and fire go round the shield.
- Lead a Static rush into him and let them wear each other down.
