# Sparker

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-sparker.png" alt="Sparker" width="240">

The Static's grenadier: hooded, with a belt of cyan-glowing EMP grenades. It never gets into the thick of it: it hangs back and lobs.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Market's west lanes and General Volt's backup, and Halcyon Tower with the break-ins from floor 2 up |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Speed** | 50 px/s on its beat, 110 getting where it wants to be |
| **Sight** | 600 px, a 100 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 200 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **EMP grenade**: 26 shock damage at the middle down to a third of it at the edge, 110 px round, thrown up to 440 px every 3.6 s

## Abilities

- Keeps its distance: backs off nearer than 230 px and closes in further than 400 px.
- Raises a grenade for 0.6 s before it throws; a cyan ring on the ground shows where it will land.
- Caught within about 38 px of the middle, the jolt stuns you. Only players on foot are hurt: a car keeps you out of it.
- Its EMPs knock Halcyon's watcher drones, sting drones and turrets out for 5 s; it goes after the watchers in the feud.

## How to beat it

- Move off the cyan ring before the grenade comes down.
- Rush it: it backs off rather than fight up close, and has little health.
- Lead it under Halcyon's drones and turrets and let it switch them off for you.
