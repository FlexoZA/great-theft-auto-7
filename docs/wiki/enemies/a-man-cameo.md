# A-Man, passing through

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/a-man-cameo.png" alt="A-Man, passing through" width="240">

A man in a suit, round glasses and a very new moustache. He blinks in near you, snaps his briefcase open, leaves a horde of sentry turrets and blinks out again. A warning: the fight comes later.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | City 17's plaza, the first time anyone walks into it |
| **Health** | can't be hurt here |
| **Visits** | 3, 8 s apart |
| **Turrets** | 5 a visit for one player, more with more |

## Abilities

- Lands 170 to 280 px from somebody in the plaza, opens the case 0.7 s later and is gone 0.6 s after that.
- The Hunter-Chopper can call him in on any map of his trail: then his case lets out its Hunters instead.

## How to beat it

- Don't waste rounds on him: knock the turrets over instead.
- The fight with him is at the top of the Citadel.
