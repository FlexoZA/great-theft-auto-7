# Shotgun

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Shotgun's Bluff

<img src="img/shotgun-boss.png" alt="Shotgun" width="240">

A sniper who hates debating and hates his rifle almost as much. He shoots down from the top of the cliff, vanishes, and turns up somewhere else along the lip.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Shotgun's Bluff, on the plateau above the meadow |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 1600 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 8 sniper rounds or 80 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 105 px/s hurrying to a new spot, 42 winded |
| **Sight** | 1800 px |
| **Drops** | 35 koins and his chicken ability ([chicken](../abilities/chicken.md)) |

## Weapons

- **Sniper rifle**: 200 damage a round, 5 rounds then a 5 s reload, out to about 1900 px
- **Pistol**: 20 damage a round, a round every 0.4 s, for anyone who gets within 320 px

## Abilities

- Draws a bead before every shot: a laser from his rifle and a ring on the target for 1 s, his aim leading you until the last 0.25 s, then the round.
- Vanishes like a chicken for 6 s (again 15 s later) and walks to a new spot while out of sight. He can still be hit while hidden.
- Get within 320 px and he vanishes; when he can't, he backs off firing his pistol.
- The cliff stops every round fired up at him from the meadow, but not his own coming down.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, out of sight if he can, for 200 health.
- Running and vanishing cost him breath: winded, he walks and stays where you can see him.

## How to beat it

- When the ring closes on you, stop, turn or duck behind something: the shot goes where his aim was.
- Cross the meadow from cover to cover and take the ramp at the far left up to the plateau.
- After 5 rounds he has a 5 s reload: that's the time to move.
- A freeze holds him still, and a stink sends him running.

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
