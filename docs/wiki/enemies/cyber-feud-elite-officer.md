# Elite officer

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-elite-officer.png" alt="Elite officer" width="240">

A Halcyon officer in black and gold, the best the company has: tougher, with a harder-hitting rifle fired in longer bursts.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Roof: one with each of the dropship's drops from the second on |
| **Health** | 180 |
| **Speed** | 62 px/s walking, 48 on a beat, 90 closing in |
| **Sight** | 640 px |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **AR**: 22.5 damage a round, in bursts of 6, then 0.8 s

## Abilities

- 3 times an officer's health, and his rounds hit 1.5 times as hard.
- Comes down the dropship's ropes with the rest of the drop and walks a beat round the relay it is guarding.
- Calls in up to 3 of his own side within 800 px when he spots you, as any officer.

## How to beat it

- Drop him first: his bursts are the ones that hurt.
- He turns at only 2 rad/s: sprint across his cone and he loses you.
