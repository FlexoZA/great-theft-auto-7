# Brute

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-brute.png" alt="Brute" width="240">

The Static's heavy: a big man in a rust-orange work jacket and a welding mask, with a giant chrome arm and a cyan reactor in his chest. Slow, and he soaks up fire for the pack.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Halcyon Tower with the Static's break-ins from floor 3 up, and the Roof: up the stairs while Kilowatt fights, and with the dropship |
| **Health** | 150 |
| **Speed** | 45 px/s on his beat, 85 going in, 400 charging |
| **Sight** | 520 px, a 100 degree cone, and he hears anyone within 200 px |
| **Drops** | 8 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Charge**: 34 impact damage to whoever is on his line, about 300 px long, every 4 s
- **Punch**: 24 impact damage within 36 px, wound up for 0.35 s, every 1.3 s

## Abilities

- Between 70 and 280 px off you, braces for 0.7 s with a cyan line on the ground showing where he will go, then charges down it.
- Charging into a wall dazes him for 1.2 s.
- His arm is breachable: on foot within 70 px of him, hold the action key for 1.5 s and it locks up. For 4 s he spins, swinging at whatever is next to him for 30 impact damage every 0.45 s, his own side and you too (the blows are yours). Let go and the breach drains away.

## How to beat it

- Step sideways off the cyan line while he braces.
- Get him to charge into a wall, then unload while he is dazed.
- Breach his arm in the middle of his pack and step back.
