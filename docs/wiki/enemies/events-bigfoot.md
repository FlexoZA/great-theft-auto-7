# Bigfoot (city event)

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / The city

<img src="img/events-bigfoot.png" alt="Bigfoot (city event)" width="240">

The forest's Bigfoot loose in the streets. He goes for the nearest player, or their buildings when nobody is close, swiping, leaping onto you, and bringing a litter of squirrels.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The city, as an event once the job "The truth is out there (in the woods)" is done: he comes out onto a road away from everyone |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 2500 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever hits him: 125 hits bring him down |
| **Speed** | 120 px/s chasing, 40 winded |
| **Drops** | 60 koins, and a legendary bigfoot leap |

## Weapons

- **Swipe**: 16 melee damage, one every 1.4 s; 25 to a building
- **Leap**: every 5 s, up to 620 px: 30 impact damage to everyone inside 125 px of where he lands, and 60 to the buildings under it

## Abilities

- Players within 900 px come before any building.
- He crouches for 0.6 s, then a ring on the ground shows where he comes down, 1 s before he lands.
- Leaps over whatever he is stuck behind after 2 s getting no closer.
- Brings 15 squirrels at a time; 4 s after the last one bursts he roars up another litter.
- Under 40% health he goes for a medkit within 700 px, which puts back 200.
- He tires: chasing spends his breath, and winded he lumbers and can't leap.

## How to beat it

- Bullets pass under him while he is in the air: shoot him as he lands.
- Get out of the ring before he comes down.
- Wear him out: winded, he lumbers at 40 px/s and a walk leaves him behind.
- A freeze holds him on the ground, and a stink cloud sends him off for a moment.

---

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players._
