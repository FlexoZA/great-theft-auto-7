# Watcher drone

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-watcher.png" alt="Watcher drone" width="240">

A small flying disc with a magenta eye and no gun. It finds you, and Halcyon's officers come.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Over the Market's stalls, and in Halcyon Tower's lobby and offices |
| **Health** | 20 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s on its beat, 120 following somebody |
| **Sight** | 440 px, a 70 degree cone, over the stalls |
| **Drops** | 4 koins |

## Abilities

- Sees over walls outdoors; in the tower only who is in the same room. Seeing you, it hangs 140 px off you and calls in up to 3 of Halcyon's officers within 1100 px, again every 6 s while it has you.
- Out of its cone for 2 s, you are gone and it flies back to its beat.
- A sparker's EMP knocks it out for 5 s: it sinks and goes dark.
- Only players' rounds reach it: the factions' pass under it.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it down before it calls: it has little health.
- Get out of its cone, or out of the room in the tower.
