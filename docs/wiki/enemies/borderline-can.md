# Rim cannon

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-can.png" alt="Rim cannon" width="240">

A heavy twin-barrelled gun on a turret, bolted to the Bunker's rim.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Bunker: eleven round the rim of its roof; the way on stays shut until every one is down |
| **Health** | 120 |
| **Reach** | 1600 px, all the way round, over anything in the way |
| **Drops** | 8 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shell**: lobbed at the nearest player every 4.5 to 7.5 s, landing 1.5 s later on a red ring within 45 px of where they stood: 26 explosive damage, 75 px blast

## Abilities

- Its muzzle glows for 0.9 s before a shell goes. It turns slowly and never moves.

## How to beat it

- Watch for the red rings and keep moving: standing still is how a shell finds you.
- Work round the rim: the further one is, the longer you have to step out of its ring.
