# Arc hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-arc.png" alt="Arc hauler" width="240">

Blue coils on both arms and a dish on its back that throws a dome over it and its friends.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Standing watch in the Refinery's tunnels and in Thousand Papercuts |
| **Health** | 80 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s on its beat, 105 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 520 px, a 80 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 170 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shock bolts**: bursts of 2, 0.25 s apart, 1.2 to 2 s between bursts; one from each arm, 10 shock damage, and 6 a second for 1.5 s after

## Abilities

- Every 11 to 14 s in a fight its dish throws a dome 180 px round it for 5 s: rounds from outside stop at it, and anyone inside takes 8 shock damage a second.
- Keeps its distance, closer than most: closes in past 380 px, backs off inside 200.
- Seeing somebody, it calls the army within 520 px to come and look.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Wait the dome out, or walk in and shoot from inside it.
- From inside the dome your shots hit everything under it, blasts too; from outside nothing does.
