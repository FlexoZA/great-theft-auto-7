# Kilowatt (on the Overpass)

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-kilowatt-cameo.png" alt="Kilowatt (on the Overpass)" width="240">

The Static's boss, standing on the back of a war-rig as it tears up the road past you, shouting. He is not fought here: that is for the Roof.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Overpass, roaring past on his own rig 11 s after you arrive, or once somebody drives off |
| **Speed** | 560 px/s up the road |

## Abilities

- Rides flat out up the road for 3.4 s, then the rig swerves into the crash barrier and goes up.
- He leaps off onto the road just before it does, laughs, and 2.4 s later runs for the edge and jumps off the overpass.
- He can't be hurt here: nothing of his takes a round.

## How to beat it

- Keep out of his rig's way and enjoy the show: the Overpass's music starts as he goes by.
