# Squirrel

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Whispering Pines

<img src="img/alien-hunt-squirrel.png" alt="Squirrel" width="240">

An alien spy, according to Wendell. It zigzags out of the trees at him, bites and hops back, and he won't go on until it is down.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Whispering Pines, out of the trees at each of the first four clearings |
| **Health** | 30 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 2 hits |
| **Speed** | 150 px/s |
| **Numbers** | One at a clearing, one after another for every player |
| **Drops** | Sometimes something to pick up |

## Weapons

- **Bite**: 7 damage to Wendell, one every 0.8 s. It never bites you.

## Abilities

- Bites Wendell (100 health) flat and he faints for 4 s, and another squirrel comes for him.

## How to beat it

- Stay by Wendell and shoot it on its way in: it zigzags, so lead it.
- A freeze holds it still.
