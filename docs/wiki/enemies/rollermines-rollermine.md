# Rollermine

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/rollermines-rollermine.png" alt="Rollermine" width="240">

The Combine's mine that hunts: a steel ball half sunk in the ground until you come near, then it hops out, blades snapping open, and rolls after you faster than you can run.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Sunk in the ground on the Winding Road and in the Citadel; A-Man's finale sets more down already awake |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Speed** | up to 260 px/s, picking up 520 px/s every second; a car flat out gets away, slowly |
| **Sight** | wakes when you come within 460 px, chases you from up to 1600 px |
| **Drops** | 1 koin when you shoot it |

## Weapons

- **Blast**: goes off when it touches you or your car: 45 explosive damage at the middle, falling off to 95 px; it hurts everyone and every car near it
- **Shock**: somebody on foot it touches takes 35 shock damage first, enough to stun them

## Abilities

- Beeps once it is within 140 px of you: it is about to touch you.
- Leads a moving target, and has momentum: it overshoots a turn, swings round and bounces off walls.
- Caught in a blast, it goes off too 0.18 s later, so a cluster goes up in a chain.
- With nobody to chase for 7 s it rolls to a stop, blades out, and wakes again when you come near.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it before it reaches you: it goes off where it is, and a blast nearby sets it off.
- Sidestep at the last moment: it overshoots turns.
- A freeze stops it dead; a stink cloud sends it rolling off the other way for a moment.
- Drive: a car flat out pulls away from it.
