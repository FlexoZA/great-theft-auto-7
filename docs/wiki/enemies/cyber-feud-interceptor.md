# Interceptor

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

Halcyon's patrol car: a grey SUV with a magenta and blue light bar, tuned faster than it left the factory, with an officer leaning out of the window shooting.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Overpass, parked on the shoulder to pull out after whoever drives past |
| **Health** | 220 (a car's, armoured) |
| **Speed** | Up to 520 px/s chasing |
| **Sight** | Wakes for a player within 900 px in sight |
| **Numbers** | 3 for one player, 1 more for each human past the first |
| **Drops** | 8 koins when wrecked |

## Weapons

- **Ram**: drives into your side once within 200 px, then drops back for 0.9 s and comes again
- **Officer's SMG**: 9 damage a round, 0.4 s apart in bursts of 5, then 1.1 s, out to 520 px

## Abilities

- Chases flat out at where you will be in a moment, steering round walls and wrecks.
- Lets you go after 5 s out of sight or 1800 px away, and pulls over to wait for the next one.
- In a flare-up between the two sides the Static's war-rigs ram it too.

## How to beat it

- Wreck it like any car: the officer goes with it.
- Start a flare-up between the two sides: the Static's war-rigs go for it then.
