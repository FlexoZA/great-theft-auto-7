# VEX

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-vex.png" alt="VEX" width="240">

The voice in your earpiece the whole way, and the one who wanted the Shard all along. She locks the roof, comes up huge, her face projected over all of it, and turns the tower's turrets on you. She can't be shot: you beat her by breaching the relays that keep her there.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Roof, once Saye and Kilowatt are down and the Shard is in her uplink |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Numbers** | 4 turrets for one player, 1 more for each human past the first |

## Weapons

- **Shock pulse**: a ring of 18 shock damage out to 190 px round the relay she is at, every 6 s
- **Her turrets**: sentry turrets that come up round the roof when she takes it (Sentry turret's page)

## Abilities

- 12 s after the Shard is handed over she takes the roof: a green barrier drops across the stairs.
- Her relays: on foot within 75 px of one, hold the action key for 5 s to breach it. Let go and after 1.5 s it drains away.
- She sits at one of the relays left, moving to another every 20 s; breaching the one she is at takes twice as long.
- Before each pulse her relay charges for 2 s, its reach lighting up blue and whining: the tell. From then until 1 s after the pulse it keeps its breach with nobody at it.
- Every relay that falls, her face breaks up more, and Halcyon's dropship comes at once if it isn't in the air.
- On the last relay she gets away into the net: the roof unlocks and her turrets go dead.
- Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), her pulse hits 15% harder.

## How to beat it

- Step out of the blue ring when it lights up and back in after the pulse: you lose nothing.
- Work the relays she isn't at: they go twice as fast.
- Breach or shoot her turrets first if they have the relay covered.
