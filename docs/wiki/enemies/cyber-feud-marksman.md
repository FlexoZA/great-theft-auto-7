# Marksman

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-marksman.png" alt="Marksman" width="240">

Halcyon's sharpshooter: a dark helmet and a long rifle. A thin magenta line shows where the next round is going.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Up on the Overpass's gantries, and in the far corners of Halcyon Tower's guard and executive floors |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Sight** | 950 px, a 130 degree cone; over everything from a gantry, only along a clear line in the tower |
| **Numbers** | 3 for one player, 1 more for each human past the first |
| **Drops** | 4 koins |

## Weapons

- **Rifle**: 40 damage, one round at 1700 px/s, out to 950 px; then 2.4 s working the bolt

## Abilities

- His line follows you for 1 s, leading a car to where it will be, then holds still and bright for 0.4 s before he fires down it.
- In a flare-up on the Overpass he shoots the Static too, and they him.

## How to beat it

- Swerve or step aside the moment the line holds still: you have 0.4 s.
- Shoot back while he works the bolt (2.4 s).
