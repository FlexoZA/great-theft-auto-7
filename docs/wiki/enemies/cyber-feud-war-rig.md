# War-rig

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

The Static's battering ram: a rusty truck tractor with a spiked chrome ram on the nose, junk plates welded all over it, and a ganger with a shotgun riding on the back.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Overpass, parked on the shoulder between Halcyon's interceptors |
| **Health** | 380 (a car's, junk welded all over it) |
| **Speed** | Up to 500 px/s |
| **Sight** | Wakes for something to hit within 850 px in sight |
| **Numbers** | 2 for one player, 1 more for each human past the first |
| **Drops** | 10 koins when wrecked |

## Weapons

- **Ram**: comes at you nose first, flat out, then drops back for 1.2 s after a hit and comes again
- **Ganger's shotgun**: 6 pellets of 9 damage a pull, every 1.3 s, out to 260 px

## Abilities

- While the two sides are at it on the Overpass, it rams Halcyon's interceptors too.
- Lets you go after 5 s out of sight or 1700 px away, and pulls over to wait.

## How to beat it

- It aims at where you will be 0.3 s on: change direction as it closes.
- Get it into a flare-up: it goes for the interceptors then.
