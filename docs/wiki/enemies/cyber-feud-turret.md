# Sentry turret

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-turret.png" alt="Sentry turret" width="240">

A grey tripod with a magenta lens. It sweeps its arc, puts a laser on whoever it sees, and fires down it. Get next to one and it can be yours.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Bolted down in Halcyon Tower's labs and round its security floor; on the Roof, dropped by Saye and driven by VEX |
| **Health** | 80 |
| **Sight** | 620 px, a 50 degree cone sweeping its arc, along a clear line |
| **Numbers** | On the Roof, up to 3 of Saye's at once, and 4 of VEX's for one player (1 more for each human past the first) |
| **Drops** | 5 koins |

## Weapons

- **Burst fire**: 8 damage a round, in bursts of 4 (0.09 s apart), then 0.9 s, out to 620 px

## Abilities

- Its laser is on you for 0.6 s before the first round; it turns at 2.2 rad/s and never past the ends of its arc.
- Breachable: on foot within 60 px of it, hold the action key for 2.5 s and it fights for you for 15 s, shooting Halcyon and the Static (the kills are yours). Let go and the breach drains away.
- A sparker's EMP knocks it out for 5 s.
- Only players' rounds reach it.

## How to beat it

- Come at it from behind, out of its arc, and breach it.
- Cross its front fast up close: at 2.2 rad/s you outrun its turn.
