# Sentry turret

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/a-man-cameo-turret.png" alt="Sentry turret" width="240">

White pods on three thin legs with one red eye and a laser sight, a horde of them at a time. They aren't clever: each scuttles about at random and now and then sprays a burst in any old direction.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Out of A-Man's briefcase: City 17's plaza, the top of the Citadel and his city event |
| **Health** | 1 |
| **Hits** | one round of anything knocks one over |
| **Speed** | 110 px/s |
| **Numbers** | 5 out of the case on each plaza visit, 8 at the top of the Citadel, 8 in his city event, for one player (more with more) |

## Weapons

- **Uzi rounds**: 6 damage a round, bursts of 4, 0.6 to 1.6 s apart, in whatever direction it picks

## Abilities

- Falls over by itself after 20 s.
- Nobody is aimed at, but the rounds are real: they hurt whoever they meet. Never the Combine, though.

## How to beat it

- Anything knocks one over: spray them.
- Keep out of their way until they fall over by themselves.
- A freeze stops them where they stand, guns and all.
