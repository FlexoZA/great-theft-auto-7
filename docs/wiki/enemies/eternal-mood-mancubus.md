# The Mancouchbus

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-mancubus.png" alt="The Mancouchbus" width="240">

A huge fat demon who sat down in a recliner when the world ended and never got up again. Heck put the recliner on tank treads and his arms into a pair of rocket launchers. The gate stays closed until he is down.

## Weapons

- **Volley**: arms up, whining, for 0.8 s, then a rocket from each arm in turn, 3 pairs (one more under half health), 0.28 s apart; every 2.6 to 3.4 s (1.8 to 2.5 under half)
- **Sweep**: arms up wide for 1 s, then 7 rockets in a fan, gaps between them; 35% of his attacks, 50% under half health
- **Rockets**: 40 explosive damage within 100 px of where each goes off, flying at 430 px/s for about 946 px
- **Bump**: within 130 px: he leans back for 0.55 s (a ring on the road), then lurches forward: 35 impact damage to everyone within 120 px of his front, knocked 170 px; at most every 2.4 s

## Abilities

- Sits about 380 px from you, rolling in or backing off, and keeps to the Boulevard.
- At two thirds and a third of his health he shouts for the lads: 2 Cacodaemons come in over the roofs (more with more players).
- Under 40% health he rolls off for a snack break: the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- Rolling at full speed costs him breath: about 13 s of it and he's winded, crawling.
- Gets out from under abilities.

## How to beat it

- Get behind him: he is slow to turn, and his back takes the most damage.
- Keep moving across a volley, and look for the gaps in a sweep.
- Off the Boulevard he won't follow: he rolls back to his spot.
- A freeze holds him stiff.
