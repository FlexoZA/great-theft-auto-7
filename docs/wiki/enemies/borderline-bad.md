# Badass hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-bad.png" alt="Badass hauler" width="240">

The elite: big, black and yellow, horns either side of its eye, a war cannon on each arm and rocket pods on its back. It doesn't mind you close.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | One a level from Prosperity on, with drones tending it |
| **Health** | 450 |
| **Speed** | 55 px/s on its beat, 85 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 700 px, a 90 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 200 px |
| **Drops** | 15 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **War cannons**: bursts of 3, 0.18 s apart, 1.4 to 2.2 s between bursts; shells, 14 explosive damage within 45 px
- **Pods**: every 9 to 12 s its pods glow for 0.9 s, then 6 rockets come down on red rings round you, 26 explosive damage each within 80 px
- **Stomp**: within 88 px it rears up for 0.5 s and stamps: 25 impact damage to everyone round it, knocked down; at most every 4 s

## Abilities

- Keeps its distance, but short: closes in past 380 px, backs off inside 160.
- Seeing somebody, it calls the army within 520 px to come and look.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Down its drones first.
- Back off past 110 px when it rears up.
- Keep moving when its pods glow.
