# Hunter-Chopper

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/hunter-chopper-chopper.png" alt="Hunter-Chopper" width="240">

The Combine's gunship. It circles the square, locks on to whoever it can see and hoses them with blue rounds, and every so often dives over you on a bombing run.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Outer City, circling the square on the island from the moment you arrive |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 3600 |
| **Speed** | 210 px/s round the square, 300 px/s on a bombing run |
| **Sight** | 700 px |
| **Drops** | 40 koins, and an SMG for every player (unlocks it in the shop) ([SMG](../weapons/smg.md)) |

## Weapons

- **Pulse gun**: bursts of 20 rounds of 6 damage, a round every 0.075 s, after a 1 s lock-on beam; 2.2 s between bursts
- **Bombs**: on a bombing run, every 16 s or so: 4 pairs, one off each side, each 40 explosive damage at the middle, falling off to 80 px, 1 s after the ring shows where it lands
- **Crash**: where it comes down it goes up: 60 explosive damage at the middle, out to 150 px

## Abilities

- At 80% health it sets down Combine soldiers under it: 3 sets of 3, 8 s apart.
- At 30% health A-Man blinks in by whoever is nearest, opens his briefcase on 3 Hunters and blinks out.
- The lock-on beam shows you where the burst is going; once it fires, the gun turns after you only slowly.
- A machine: no breath, no medkits, no dodging.

## How to beat it

- When the beam is on you, run across it: the burst can't keep up. Standing still or running straight away gets you hit.
- Cover stops its rounds, and it only fires on what it can see.
- Step off the rings as the bombs come down, and keep clear of where it falls.
- Rockets hurt it: it is big, so a blast reaches it 40 px further out than anyone else.
