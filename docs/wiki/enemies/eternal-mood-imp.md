# Imp

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-imp.png" alt="Imp" width="240">

The common demon, brown and spiky. A pack crawls out of a vent one after another, keeps its distance, strafes, and throws fireballs at you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Every Eternal Mood level, bursting out of vents, manholes and cracks as you come near |
| **Health** | 60 |
| **Speed** | 140 px/s closing in, 105 strafing |
| **Sight** | 640 px all round; seeing nobody, it hunts the nearest player within 1400 px |
| **Numbers** | A pack a vent, one out every 0.7 s, one more for each player past the first |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Fireball**: 14 fire damage, held up for 0.45 s first, one every 1.8 to 2.8 s, thrown out to 650 px at 330 px/s
- **Claws**: 10 melee damage, one every 0.8 s, within 30 px

## Abilities

- A vent bursts open the first time a player comes within about 420 px of it, and its pack crawls out (1 s each).
- Keeps between 150 and 380 px from you, strafing from side to side.
- The Baron of Heck's roar and the Spider Mattressmind's murmur bring more.

## How to beat it

- Its fireballs are slow: watch for the raised arm and step to the side.
- Wait at the vent and shoot each one as it crawls out, before it can throw.
