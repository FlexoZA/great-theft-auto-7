# Executive drone

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-saye-drone.png" alt="Executive drone" width="240">

Saye's personal guard: small drones circling her, shooting at whoever is nearest, and shielding Kilowatt when he needs it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Roof, orbiting Director Saye |
| **Health** | 30 |
| **Numbers** | 3 round her, 6 once Kilowatt is down; more on a job finished before |

## Weapons

- **Light gun**: 5 damage a round, bursts of two every 1.7 s, out to 520 px

## Abilities

- Orbits 50 px from Saye; while Kilowatt vents they circle him instead, and while any are there hits on him do no extra damage.
- Shot down, Saye calls more up 12 s later.
- Only players' rounds reach them.

## How to beat it

- Shoot them off Kilowatt when he vents: then every hit on him does 2 times the damage.
- Clear them, then go for Saye before she calls the rest back up.

---

_Numbers are for a job nobody has finished yet. Each time it has been finished before (up to 10), this enemy has 10% more health and hits 10% harder._
