# Hauler dock

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

The bay Hyperbole parks its haulers in, with a plasma gun on a turret over it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Frozen Wreck: a charging bay at each post, guarding a terminal that opens the tunnel's blast door |
| **Health** | 300 |
| **Sight** | 620 px, all the way round |
| **Drops** | 10 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Plasma turret**: bursts of 3, 0.22 s apart, 1.8 to 2.6 s between bursts; bolts at 300 px/s, 14 plasma damage, splashing 32 px

## Abilities

- With anyone within 900 px it builds a junk hauler every 20 s and sends it out, 3 of its own out at most.
- Hacking its terminal shuts it down. It never moves.

## How to beat it

- Hack the terminal beside it rather than shooting it down.
- Sidestep its bolts: they are slow.
