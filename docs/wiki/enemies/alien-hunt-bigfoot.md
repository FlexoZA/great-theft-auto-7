# Bigfoot

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Whispering Pines

<img src="img/alien-hunt-bigfoot.png" alt="Bigfoot" width="240">

Wendell wanted aliens. He got Bigfoot. Huge and hairy, he chases whoever is nearest, swipes at them, and every few seconds leaps and comes down on them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Whispering Pines, in the fifth clearing at the end of Wendell's trail |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 1800 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 90 hits |
| **Speed** | 105 px/s chasing, 40 winded |
| **Sight** | 1100 px |
| **Drops** | 40 koins |

## Weapons

- **Swipe**: 16 melee damage, one every 1.4 s
- **Leap**: 30 impact damage to everyone within 125 px of where he lands

## Abilities

- Every 5 s he leaps at anyone within 620 px: 0.6 s crouched while a ring shows where he will land, 1 s in the air, 0.8 s getting up.
- Bullets pass under him while he is in the air.
- Chasing costs him breath: winded, he lumbers along slower than you walk, with no leap in him.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, leaping onto it if he can, for 200 health.

## How to beat it

- Get out of the ring before he lands, then shoot him while he gets up.
- Sprinting outruns him.
- A freeze holds him still, and a stink sends him off.

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
