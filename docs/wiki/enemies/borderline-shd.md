# Shield drone

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-shd.png" alt="Shield drone" width="240">

A small yellow disc on four rotors. It never fights: it puts a bubble on a hauler, one in a fight first.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Hanging behind the haulers: the Bragium Pit, Prosperity, the Refinery, the Assembly Line and Thousand Papercuts |
| **Health** | 25 |
| **Speed** | 150 px/s |
| **Drops** | 1 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Abilities

- Gives the hauler it tends a 80 point bubble that soaks hits first, topped up 20 a second along its beam (150 px long); untended, a bubble fades 10 a second.
- Looks for somebody to tend within 700 px, and hangs 60 px behind them, out of your line of fire.
- Flies over walls, and anything can shoot it.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it first: it goes down fast, and what it tends goes down slow until it does.
- Get round the side of whoever it tends to get a clear shot.
