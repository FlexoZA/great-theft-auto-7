# Jet hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-jet.png" alt="Jet hauler" width="240">

A rifle hauler with wings folded on its back and thrusters under them. Every so often it takes off and bombs a line straight over you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Standing watch in Prosperity and Thousand Papercuts |
| **Health** | 70 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s on its beat, 130 in a fight, 320 in the air |
| **Sight** | 640 px, a 80 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 170 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Rifle**: bursts of 3, 0.12 s apart, 1 to 1.8 s between bursts; 7 bullet damage a shot
- **Bombing run**: every 7 to 11 s it crouches for 0.7 s as its thrusters light, then flies over you and 360 px past; 5 rockets come down along its line on red rings, 20 explosive damage each within 70 px

## Abilities

- In the air nothing reaches it, and it flies over walls.
- Lands where there is room and stands 0.5 s before it fights on.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- When its thrusters light, step off the line between you and it: the rockets fall along it.
- Shoot it as it lands.
