# Junk hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-jnk.png" alt="Junk hauler" width="240">

The cheapest thing Hyperbole ever built, and it knows it: a rusty frame with a pistol. It walks straight at you with no cover and no plan.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | In packs on the Frozen Wreck, Liar's Landing and the Bragium Pit, picking over whatever is lying about; a hauler dock builds more |
| **Health** | 30 |
| **Speed** | 22 px/s picking about, 105 after you |
| **Sight** | 480 px, a 100 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 150 px |
| **Drops** | 1 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Pistol**: bursts of 2, 0.35 s apart, 0.8 to 1.6 s between bursts; 6 bullet damage a shot

## Abilities

- The first of a pack to see you wakes the rest of it.
- Walks in to 170 px and stands there shooting.
- Loses you and goes to where you were, looks about for 6 s, and settles there.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Packs bunch up: one grenade or rocket does a lot of them.
- They don't take cover: stand back and pick them off.
