# Combine soldier

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/a-man-combine.png" alt="Combine soldier" width="240">

Masked soldiers in grey-blue armour, each with a gun picked at random. They guard checkpoints, walk beats in squads, pour out of garrisons and crew the MG nests, and talk it over on the radio.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Every map of A-Man's trail, from City 17 to the Citadel |
| **Health** | 60 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 3 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 48 px/s on a beat, 62 walking, 90 closing in |
| **Sight** | 640 px in a 60 degree cone (100 once he is on edge), and 170 px all round |
| **Numbers** | 2 at each checkpoint, squads of 3 on the beats, 4 out of a garrison, a crew of 4 to an MG nest, more with more players |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, and sometimes something to pick up |

## Weapons

- **Pistol**: 7% of them: 20 damage a round, bursts of 2, 0.9 s apart, once you are within 920 px
- **Uzi**: 15% of them: 12 damage a round, bursts of 5, 1.2 s apart, once you are within 310 px
- **SMG**: 11% of them: 13 damage a round, bursts of 6, 1.1 s apart, once you are within 420 px
- **AK-47**: 30% of them: 18 damage a round, bursts of 3, 1.1 s apart, once you are within 1020 px
- **AR**: 7% of them: 15 damage a round, bursts of 4, 1.2 s apart, once you are within 1170 px
- **Shotgun**: 15% of them: 6 pellets of 11 damage, one every 1.3 s, once you are within 220 px
- **Rocket launcher**: 4% of them: 90 explosive damage across 120 px, one every 3 s, once you are within 800 px
- **Sniper rifle**: 4% of them: 60 damage a round, one every 2.5 s, once you are within 1610 px
- **Flamethrower**: 7% of them: 5 fire damage a lick, and it sets you alight, bursts of 10, 1 s apart, once you are within 140 px
- **MG nest**: the gunner's: 12 damage a round, bursts of 14 then 1.4 s, out to 700 px, only into the nest's arc
- **Hand grenade**: 10% of them carry 1 each: 80 explosive damage across 100 px, thrown 140 to 400 px

## Abilities

- Guards stand at the checkpoints sweeping their cones; squads walk beats through City 17 and the Outer City.
- Some guard stations hold a garrison: the first time you come near, the door opens and they come out after you. On the Winding Road and in the Citadel they come in waves while you stay near.
- MG nest crews hold their places: the gunner fires only into his arc, the rest fight from cover, and when the gunner drops the nearest of them runs to the gun.
- Spots you, turns to follow you at 115 degrees a second and fires after 0.7 s to take aim.
- Calls in the nearest 3 within 800 px when he spots you; one going down sends up to 5 within 700 px to look.
- Comes after you when you duck out of sight (up to 900 px from where he started) and searches where he lost you for 5 s.
- Shot at from out of sight, he and his mates take cover and wait, then send a squad to look.
- The Hunter-Chopper sets more of them down, and so does A-Man's briefcase at the top of the Citadel.

## How to beat it

- Keep cover between you and the cones of sight, or sprint across one faster than he can turn.
- Snipe the gunner off an MG nest, then whoever runs to the gun.
- On the Winding Road a car at speed runs them down.
- A freeze stops them looking and shooting, and a stink sends them running.
