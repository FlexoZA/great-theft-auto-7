# Antlion Guard

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/antlions-guard.png" alt="Antlion Guard" width="240">

The swarm's great armoured beast. It digs up out of the sand once everything in the Coast's last section is down, then swipes, charges and screams.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Coast's point, up out of the sand once the last section is cleared |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 2400 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 120 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 190 px/s running, 120 closing in, 480 charging, 55 winded |
| **Sight** | 1600 px |
| **Drops** | 60 koins |

## Weapons

- **Swipe**: 60 impact damage up close, knocking you 110 px back
- **Charge**: 90 impact damage, and you fly 240 px
- **Scream**: up to 70 impact damage down a 64 degree cone 520 px long, blowing you back up to 320 px

## Abilities

- Paws the sand for 0.9 s before it charges from 200 to 650 px away: that's the warning.
- Runs into something mid-charge and it reels for 1.6 s.
- Rears up for 1 s before it screams, the cone showing on the ground.
- Calls for help as it is hurt: at 60% and again at 20% health a Hunter-Chopper sweeps in from behind you and drops 3 rollermines (more with more players).
- Running, charging and screaming cost it breath: winded, it walks, with no charge or scream in it.
- Under 40% health it goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.

## How to beat it

- Sidestep the charge, and better still, stand in front of a wall so it runs into it.
- Get out of the cone while it rears up.
- A freeze holds it, but only for half as long.
