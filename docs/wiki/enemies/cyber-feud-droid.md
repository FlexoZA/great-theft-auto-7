# Assassin droid

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

<img src="img/cyber-feud-droid.png" alt="Assassin droid" width="240">

Halcyon's killing machine: a lean white-plated robot with a magenta visor band, walking cloaked until it has you. It keeps its distance, strafes, and throws itself out of the way of your rounds.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Drop's last crossings before the tunnel, Halcyon Tower's server room, labs and executive floor, and the Roof with the dropship |
| **Health** | 240 |
| **Speed** | 106.25 px/s on its beat, 187.5 fighting, 143.75 strafing |
| **Sight** | 640 px, a 90 degree cone (140 on edge), and it hears anyone within 200 px |
| **Numbers** | 2 on a beat for one player, 1 more for each human past the first |
| **Drops** | 10 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Rail pistol**: 14 damage a round, in bursts of 3, then 0.9 s, out to 918 px
- **Rail shot**: 45 damage, charged for 0.9 s down a magenta sightline, every 6 s

## Abilities

- Walks cloaked until it has somebody.
- Dodges every round a player fires that would hit it, and every ability or grenade about to land on it; then it can't dodge again for 3 s.
- Keeps you between 260 and 440 px, backing off or closing in.
- Hears a shot within 550 px, and calls the others within 1000 px when it finds you.
- Doesn't heal.
- Only players' rounds hurt it: the factions' pass it by.

## How to beat it

- Make it dodge, then land a burst or two in the 3 s before it can dodge again.
- Step off the magenta sightline while the rail shot charges.
- Freeze it: a frozen droid can't dodge.
