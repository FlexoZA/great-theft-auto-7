# A-Man

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/a-man-finale.png" alt="A-Man" width="240">

The end of the trail. The same man in the same joke-shop disguise, much stronger now: he blinks straight through you, and his briefcase holds everything you met on the way up.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The top of the Citadel, once someone steps onto the top platform |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 5000 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 250 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s, never hurrying |
| **Drops** | 150 koins and his teleport ability, uncommon or better ([teleport](../abilities/teleport.md)) |

## Weapons

- **Blink**: 70 impact damage to everyone on the line he tears along, through anyone within 600 px, coming out 200 px past them
- **Briefcase**: out comes one of: 4 Combine soldiers, 2 Hunters, 4 rollermines, 7 antlions, 8 sentry turrets (for one player, more with more), never the same twice running

## Abilities

- The line of a blink shows for 0.7 s before he goes; he blinks every 2.4 to 3.9 s, breath allowing.
- Every 10% of his health you take earns a case: he holds it up for 1 s, then it snaps open.
- Under 30% health two things come out of the case at once.
- Nothing hits him mid-blink.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 1600 px, by blink if he can, for 200 health.
- Hurries out from under a player's ability about to land on him.

## How to beat it

- Step off the line while it shows.
- Deal with what comes out of the case before it piles up, and shoot him while he holds it up.
- A freeze holds him, but only for half as long; a stink throws him off the blink he was aiming.

---

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
