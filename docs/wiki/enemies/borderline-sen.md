# Sentry

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-sen.png" alt="Sentry" width="240">

A yellow turret in a hatch, out of reach until it comes up.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Flush in the floor of the Assembly Line and in Thousand Papercuts' rock |
| **Health** | 90 |
| **Sight** | 560 px, all the way round |
| **Drops** | 2 koins, maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Turret gun**: bursts of 4, 0.1 s apart, 1.2 to 1.8 s between bursts; 7 bullet damage a shot

## Abilities

- Comes up in 0.5 s when anyone is within 450 px, and sinks back 6 s after it last saw anyone.
- Sunk, nothing reaches it. It never moves.

## How to beat it

- Get behind cover as it rises, then shoot between its bursts.
