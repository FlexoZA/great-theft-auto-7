# Gun hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-gun.png" alt="Gun hauler" width="240">

Hyperbole's standard model: a rifle on one arm and a grenade launcher on the other, and drilled.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Everywhere on the line: standing watch, or walking a beat in squads |
| **Health** | 70 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s on its beat, 120 in a fight |
| **Sight** | 620 px, a 80 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 170 px |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Rifle**: bursts of 3, 0.12 s apart, 1 to 1.8 s between bursts; 8 bullet damage a shot
- **Grenade launcher**: a grenade every 9 to 14 s, at anyone 200 to 520 px off, and one where it lost you

## Abilities

- Keeps its distance: closes in past 450 px, backs off inside 250, side-stepping as it shoots.
- Seeing somebody, it calls the army within 520 px to come and look.
- Shot by somebody it can't see, it turns and goes to look.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Move after it lobs: the grenade lands where you stood.
- Squads flank in pairs: keep a wall at your back.
