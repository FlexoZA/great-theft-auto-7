# Poison Zombie

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / A-Man

<img src="img/poison-zombie-zombie.png" alt="Poison Zombie" width="240">

A man swollen and hunched under a poison headcrab, with more of them riding on his back. He shuffles after you, rakes you with his claws up close and throws his crabs at you from further off.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Winding Road, waiting on the pass at the top |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 1800 |
| **Hits** | whatever the round does: 90 pistol rounds |
| **Speed** | 58 px/s shuffling, 115 lurching, 40 winded |
| **Sight** | wakes when you come within 750 px, goes after you from 1400 px |
| **Drops** | 50 koins |

## Weapons

- **Claws**: 60 melee damage, and you bleed for 4 s
- **Headcrab**: thrown at you from 120 to 640 px away, 3 on his back and one more every 9 s

## Abilities

- Lurches when you're more than 320 px off, which costs him breath, and so does every throw.
- Never goes more than 1900 px from the pass.
- Solid to cars: drive into him at speed and he's hurt, but so is the car.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- Dodges what players drop on him.
- The Combine's rounds pass by him and his crabs.

## How to beat it

- Stay out of his claws' reach: slow as he is, one that lands hurts.
- Thin his crabs out before they reach you, and carry a medkit for the poison.
- Ramming him at 60 px/s or more hurts him, but the car loses 15 health.

---

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
