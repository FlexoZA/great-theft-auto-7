# Halcyon dropship

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Cyber Feud

A tiltrotor in Halcyon's military paint with rockets where the rotors would be. VEX calls it in to put troops down by the relay you are going for. It can't be shot: it is her call, not a fight of its own.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Over the Roof, while VEX has it |
| **Speed** | 460 px/s flat out |
| **Numbers** | 3 officers a drop for one player (more with more humans) |

## Abilities

- Comes in 12 s after VEX takes the roof, at the relay furthest along (else the one nearest a player), the spot it sets down on ringed in magenta on the ground.
- Hangs over the drop for 4 s: the officers rope down and walk a beat round the relay, and with them one of an assassin droid, an enforcer or a brute, in turn. From the second drop on, one of the officers is an elite.
- Comes again 35 s after it leaves, while fewer than 5 of what it brought (more with more humans) are still up; and at once whenever a relay falls with none in the air.

## How to beat it

- Watch for the magenta ring: be ready at that relay, or work another.
- Thin out each drop before the next: it stops coming while enough of them are up.
