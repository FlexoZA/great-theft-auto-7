# Dozer hauler

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-dzr.png" alt="Dozer hauler" width="240">

Wide, slow and tough, a bulldozer blade across its front.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Standing watch in Prosperity, the Refinery and the Assembly Line |
| **Health** | 160 |
| **Speed** | 60 px/s on its beat, 85 at you, 240 charging |
| **Sight** | 560 px, a 90 degree cone, and it hears anyone within 170 px |
| **Drops** | 6 koins, and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shove**: up close, 18 impact damage every 1.2 s, knocked down
- **Charge**: every 6 to 8 s it folds into a dozer for 0.6 s, then charges straight where you stood: 30 impact damage, flattened

## Abilities

- Its blade stops every round from the front, 35 degrees either side; blasts and fire go round it.
- It can't turn once it charges.
- A killing hit takes its legs instead, one time in 3: it drags itself on at half speed, still shooting, for 4 s, and a hit finishes it.

## How to beat it

- Flank it, or blow it up.
- Step aside when it folds down, and shoot its back as it goes by.
