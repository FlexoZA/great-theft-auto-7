# Mecha Zombie

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-mecha-zombie.png" alt="Mecha Zombie" width="240">

A zombie the UAC rebuilt: a plasma cannon for a right arm, a flamethrower and talons on the left, a red optic where an eye was. It guards the Interns while they eat, and screams the horde down on you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Fobos Base, Heck on Earth and the Foundry, walking a loop round each Intern mob |
| **Health** | 80 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s fighting, 40 on its loop |
| **Sight** | 600 px, no wall in between; awake, it hunts the nearest player within 1300 px |
| **Drops** | 4 koins, and 35% of the time a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Plasma cannon**: past 300 px: the cannon glows for 0.4 s, then 3 bolts 0.18 s apart, every 1.6 to 2.4 s; each 9 fire damage and sets you alight (1.5 s at 4 a second), flying about 780 px
- **Flamethrower**: at mid range: the pilot light flares for 0.35 s, then 1.4 s of fire, 3 fire damage a lick, 14 licks a second, reaching about 281 px and setting you alight (2 s at 6 a second); then 2 s off
- **Talons**: within 40 px: claws up for 0.3 s, then 16 melee damage, one every 0.9 s

## Abilities

- Wakes when it sees you, you shoot it, or its mob wakes. Waking, it throws its head back for 0.9 s and screams: every mob, every one of the possessed and every other Mecha Zombie within 560 px comes.
- Screams again every 18 s while it can still see somebody.
- Ranged fodder: it holds 140 to 380 px from you, side-stepping, rather than charging in.

## How to beat it

- Kill it before it sees you, or the whole room comes when it screams.
- Its plasma bolts are slow: side-step them, and keep out of its flame's reach between bursts.
- A freeze holds it stiff.
