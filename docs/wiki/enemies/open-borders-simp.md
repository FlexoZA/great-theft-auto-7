# Simp (open borders)

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / The city

<img src="img/open-borders-simp.png" alt="Simp (open borders)" width="240">

Cast open borders and 25 simps pour out round you, torches in hand. They run about lighting fires and punch anyone who comes close, the one who let them in included.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Wherever somebody casts the open borders ability, round the one who cast it |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever hits them: 2 hits down one |
| **Speed** | about 115 px/s |
| **Sight** | 90 px |
| **Numbers** | 25 a cast, for 30 s |
| **Drops** | 1 koin each, like a pedestrian |

## Weapons

- **Punch**: 5 melee damage, one every 1.1 s, to you on foot or your car
- **Torch**: a fire at their feet every 1.2 to 2.4 s; each burns for 30 s and does 5 fire damage every 0.5 s to anything touching it, and a player on foot burns on for 3 s at 5 a second

## Abilities

- Chases and punches anyone within 90 px, the caster too.
- After landing a punch, runs off for 2 to 4 s before picking on anyone again.
- Keeps within 260 px of the caster, following them about, and is gone when the 30 s are up; the fires burn on.
- Their own fires don't hurt them.

## How to beat it

- A car at 90 px/s or more flattens one.
- They follow the one who cast it, but on foot: a car leaves them behind.
- Freezing and stink work on them.
