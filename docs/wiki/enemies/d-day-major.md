# Major Looz'er

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / D-Day

<img src="img/d-day-major.png" alt="Major Looz'er" width="240">

Big, slow and never done talking about his country. He sees all round him, marches to rifle range of the nearest player and fires bursts, and throws MG nests down in front of him.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Looz'er Beach, on the hilltop behind the flag once someone reaches it |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 2200 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 110 hits |
| **Speed** | 72 px/s marching, 40 winded |
| **Sight** | 760 px, all the way round |
| **Drops** | 40 koins |

## Weapons

- **AK-47**: bursts of 3 rounds, 18 damage each, a burst every 2 s
- **MG nest**: every 11 s (the first 4 s in): a machine gun that sprays a 45 degree arc in front of him for 5 s, 18 damage a round

## Abilities

- Keeps about 260 px from whoever he is after.
- Marching costs him breath: winded, he strolls, and has no MG nest in him until it's back.
- Under 40% health he goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- Can't be hurt until his portrait is gone and the fight is on.

## How to beat it

- Stay out of the nest's arc: it only fires the way it faces.
- Run him dry and he can't keep up, or put a nest down.
- A freeze holds him still, and a stink sends him running.
