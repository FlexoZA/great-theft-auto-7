# Zombie guard

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-guard.png" alt="Zombie guard" width="240">

The UAC's security, possessed, and the one that fights smart. Posted, it sweeps its gaze across the doorway it watches; in a fight it ducks behind something solid, steps out to shoot and ducks back.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Fobos Base, Heck on Earth and the Foundry, watching doorways and walking patrols |
| **Health** | 60 |
| **Speed** | 75 px/s after you, 45 on patrol |
| **Sight** | 400 px ahead, 80 degrees across (110 on edge), 160 px all round |
| **Drops** | 3 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Shotgun**: 6 pellets of 7 damage a pull, one pull every 1.4 to 1.9 s, out to 400 px
- **Grenade**: 35% of guards carry one: 80 explosive damage within 100 px, thrown at anyone 140 to 400 px off, never near the rest of the horde

## Abilities

- Fights from cover within 170 px of where it is, out for 1.4 to 2.2 s at a time to shoot, back in for 1.2 to 2.6 s.
- Shot by somebody it can't see, it goes to ground, and the others who know to within 400 px with it. After 8 s with no more shots, up to 3 of them go out to sweep where the shots came from.
- Never goes more than 560 px from its post.
- Lost you a moment ago? A guard with a grenade may lob it where you were, within 4 s.
- Spotting you, it groans: the horde within 380 px comes to look. One going down sends those within 520 px to where he fell.
- Its rounds pass through the rest of Heck: nothing of Heck's hurts anything else of Heck's.

## How to beat it

- Flank it: its cover only faces the way it thinks you are.
- Keep moving when you hear the grenade call, and don't stand still behind a crate it knows about.
