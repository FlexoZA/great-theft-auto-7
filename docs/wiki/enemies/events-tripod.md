# The Tripod

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / The city

<img src="img/events-tripod.png" alt="The Tripod" width="240">

A war machine on three long legs, taller than the buildings. It strides over them after you, burns you with its heat ray and snatches you up into the cage at its back.

|---|---|

## Weapons

- **Lightning**: for 8 s before it rises, bolts round every player: 35 shock damage on foot, 45 to a car, 50 px round each strike, marked 0.7 s before it hits
- **Heat ray**: warms up on you for 2 s, then burns the spot 68 px across for 1.4 s: 120 fire damage a second on foot, 160 to a car
- **Sweep**: lights up an arc 260 px out in front for 1 s, then burns along it over 1.8 s: about 120 fire damage on foot as it passes
- **Tentacles**: a player on foot within 130 px is lashed at, with 0.7 s to get clear, and caged: 20 melee damage a second for up to 7 s

## Abilities

- Whoever the ray catches on foot burns on for 4 s at 15 a second, unless they dodge.
- It lets the caged player go once it has taken 8% of its health since the grab.
- Steps straight over buildings once it has somebody in sight.
- Under 40% health it strides to a medkit within 900 px, which puts back 200.
- It tires: striding, the ray and the tentacles spend its breath, and winded it only ambles and does neither.
- Nothing hurts it during the storm.

## How to beat it

- The ray stops following you 0.35 s before it fires: dash or sprint out of the ring then.
- Get off the lit arc before the sweep comes.
- Don't stand under it on foot. If a friend is caged, shoot the head to make it let go.
- It strides after you at 95 px/s: a sprint gets away, a walk doesn't.
- A freeze stops it, ray and tentacles with it.
