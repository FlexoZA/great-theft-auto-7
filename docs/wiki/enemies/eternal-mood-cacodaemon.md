# Cacodaemon

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Eternal Mood

<img src="img/eternal-mood-cacodaemon.png" alt="Cacodaemon" width="240">

A floating red tomato with one green eye and a big mouth: the heaviest thing in the horde. It drifts round you at a distance, flies straight over walls and lava, and spits big balls of fire.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Heck on Earth, the Foundry and Heck, floating over roofs and lava; the Mancouchbus and the Spider Mattressmind call more |
| **Health** | 220 |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s |
| **Sight** | 700 px, over everything; once awake it hunts the nearest player within 1600 px |
| **Drops** | 8 koins, and maybe a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Spit**: 24 fire damage, mouth open for 0.5 s first, one every 2.6 to 3.8 s, out to 640 px at 260 px/s
- **Bite**: 30 melee damage, one every 1.2 s, when you get within 24 px of its edge

## Abilities

- Wakes when you come near, when it sees you across open ground, or when it is hurt: then its whole flock comes.
- Keeps between 200 and 400 px from you, circling slowly.
- Flies: walls, roofs and lava are nothing to it.

## How to beat it

- Shoot it in the open mouth: rounds do 1.5 times the damage, and it reels back for 0.9 s without spitting.
- Its spit is big and slow: a step to the side does it.
- Don't stand under it: up close it bites.
