# The Smelter

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / borderline

<img src="img/borderline-sml.png" alt="The Smelter" width="240">

A squat plasma furnace on four legs with molten Bragium in its belly, Hyperbole's yellow band round its shell and a spout at its front.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The Refinery: it climbs out of the casting pit at the outflow when the last bridge comes down, and the freight door stays shut until it goes out |
| **Rank** | Boss |
| **Health** | 3500 |
| **Speed** | 50 px/s, 30 winded |
| **Sight** | 850 px |
| **Drops** | 45 koins and a pickup |

## Weapons

- **Plasma bolt**: its coils glow for 0.6 s, then one bolt, 320 px/s, every 1.6 to 2.3 s: 18 plasma damage, splashing 40 px
- **Gush**: every 7 to 9 s: a lane 520 px long lights up in front of it for 1 s, then it pours molten Bragium down it for 2.2 s, 40 plasma a second to whoever is in it, leaving puddles burning 5 s

## Abilities

- After every 3 moves it vents for 3 s: it stands still with its belly open, and every hit on it does 2x.
- Under 40% health it goes for the nearest medkit within 700 px, for 200 health.
- Plasma melts armor: while you're melted, what you wear stops half as much.

## How to beat it

- Get out of the lit lane before it pours, and stay off the puddles it leaves.
- Count its moves: on the third it vents. Unload into it then.
- Its bolts are slow: sidestep them.

_Health is for one player: it is multiplied by the number of players. Each time the job has been finished before (up to 10), it has 25% more health and hits 15% harder._
