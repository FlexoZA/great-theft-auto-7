# Simp

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Enemies](README.md) / Karen's Cul-de-sac

<img src="img/karen-simp.png" alt="Simp" width="240">

Karen's hangers-on, in grey hoodies, each with a name that starts "Simpin". They run at you from all sides and punch.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Karen's Cul-de-sac, turning up round her while she stands |
| **Health** | 40 |
| **Hits** | 20 a hit, whatever the gun: 2 hits |
| **Speed** | 70 px/s milling about, 135 chasing |
| **Sight** | 900 px |
| **Numbers** | 5 at a time for one player (more for more players, and each time the job has been finished), one every 4 s while Karen stands |
| **Drops** | A koin like a pedestrian, and sometimes something to pick up |

## Weapons

- **Punch**: 8 melee damage, one every 1 s

## Abilities

- Go for whoever shot Karen in the last 6 s.
- Spread round you and close in from all sides rather than queueing up.
- No more come once Karen is down, but the ones already out keep swinging.

## How to beat it

- A car at 90 px/s or more flattens one.
- A stink sends them running.
