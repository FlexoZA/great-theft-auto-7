# Shield wall

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Abilities](README.md) / Shield wall

<img src="img/shieldwall.png" alt="shield wall" width="160">

A curved wall in front of you that stops enemy fire for a few seconds. Your own rounds and your friends' go straight through.

**How to use it:** Press its key, then click to put it down facing the cursor.
