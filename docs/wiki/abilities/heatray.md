# Heat ray

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Abilities](README.md) / Heat ray

<img src="img/heatray.png" alt="heat ray" width="160">

The tripod's heat ray. Tap to aim, hold to switch between beam and sweep. Only won by beating it.

**How to use it:** Press its key, then click where to land.

|---|---|
| **Kind** | active (a key) |
| **Modes** | beam, sweep |

## How to get it

- Never in the shop: only won.
- Dropped by [The Tripod](../enemies/events-tripod.md) (The city): 60 koins, and its heat ray in a random tier (legendary 5 times in a hundred).
