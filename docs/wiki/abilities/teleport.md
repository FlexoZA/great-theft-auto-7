# Teleport

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Abilities](README.md) / Teleport

<img src="img/teleport.png" alt="teleport" width="160">

Vanish and reappear far away, tearing through anyone in between. Only won by beating A-Man.

**How to use it:** Press its key, then click where to land.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | active (a key) |
| **Works** | on foot only |

## How to get it

- Never in the shop: only won.
- Dropped by [A-Man (city event)](../enemies/a-man-event.md) (The city): 50 koins, and his teleport in a random tier (legendary 3 times in a hundred).
- Dropped by [A-Man](../enemies/a-man-finale.md) (A-Man): 150 koins and his teleport ability, uncommon or better.
