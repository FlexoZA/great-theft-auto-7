# Chicken

[Wiki](../README.md) / [Abilities](README.md) / Chicken

<img src="img/chicken.png" alt="chicken" width="160">

Go invisible until you shoot: nobody sees you and nothing hunts you. Stray rounds still hurt.

**How to use it:** Press its key: it goes off on you.
